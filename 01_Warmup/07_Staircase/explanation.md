# Staircase

## Problem Statement & URL
- **URL:** https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/staircase/1
- **Topic:** 01_Warmup
- **Difficulty:** Easy

## Approach & Logic
For line i from 1 to n, print (n-i) leading spaces followed by i '#' characters.

## Complexity Analysis
- **Time Complexity:** O(N)
- **Space Complexity:** O(1)
