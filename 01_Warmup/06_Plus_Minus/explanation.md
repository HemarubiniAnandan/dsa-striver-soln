# Plus Minus

## Problem Statement & URL
- **URL:** https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/plus-minus/1
- **Topic:** 01_Warmup
- **Difficulty:** Easy

## Approach & Logic
Count positive, negative, and zero values in array, then divide by total count n and format to 6 decimal places.

## Complexity Analysis
- **Time Complexity:** O(N)
- **Space Complexity:** O(1)
