# Armstrong Numbers

## Problem Information
- **URL:** https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/armstrong-numbers2727/1
- **Topic:** 01_Basics
- **Difficulty:** Easy

## Approach & Logic
Sum each digit raised to the power of total number of digits. Compare with original n.

## Complexity Analysis
- **Time Complexity:** O(log10 N)
- **Space Complexity:** O(1)
