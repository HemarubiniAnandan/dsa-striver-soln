# Sum of 1 to n Divisors

## Problem Information
- **URL:** https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/sum-of-all-divisors-from-1-to-n4738/1
- **Topic:** 01_Basics
- **Difficulty:** Easy

## Approach & Logic
Each integer i between 1 and N appears as a divisor (N // i) times across all numbers from 1 to N.

## Complexity Analysis
- **Time Complexity:** O(N)
- **Space Complexity:** O(1)
